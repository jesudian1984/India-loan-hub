# Transfer IndiaLoanHub to your friend's Lovable account

## Goal
Move project ownership to your friend while keeping the project and its current work intact.

## Steps
1. Invite your friend's Lovable email to the current workspace as an administrator.
2. Open the project settings and choose **Project → General → Transfer ownership**.
3. Select your friend and confirm the transfer.
4. Ask your friend to set up billing and credits on their account, because these do not transfer.
5. Confirm that your friend can open, edit, preview, and publish the project.
6. If Lovable shows a backend connection warning, follow the transfer dialog's instructions before completing the transfer.
7. Optionally ask your friend to invite you back as an editor or administrator after ownership changes.

## Important notes
- I cannot complete the account-level transfer from inside the project chat.
- Transfer ownership gives your friend control of the project; your access may end unless they invite you back.
- Do not use public remixing for this purpose if you need the same project, backend, leads, and settings; remixing creates an independent copy.
